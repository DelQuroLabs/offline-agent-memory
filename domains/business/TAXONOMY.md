# Business operations and content creation: topic map

These are suggested tags and knowledge areas, not extra security scopes. Use a small vocabulary consistently.

| Tag | Contents |
|---|---|
| `strategy` | Goals, assumptions, options, and experiments |
| `operations` | Standard operating procedures and responsibility maps |
| `content` | Briefs, drafts, approved claims, and channel conventions |
| `analytics` | Metric definitions and reviewed findings |
| `research` | Local audience and market source collections |
| `reference` | Approved business and brand reference material |

Add a project tag when useful, plus a topic and a record kind. Avoid dozens of near-duplicate tags. Preserve source dates and versions in record bodies, not only in tags.
