# Share between domains deliberately

Use sharing only when another domain benefits from a specific reusable conclusion.

1. Identify the minimum claim or procedure to share and its destination uses.
2. Check the original classification and whether the source contains unrelated private material.
3. For active, current public/internal memory, use `share-draft` to create a new shared draft.
4. For private material, author a separate redacted summary. Do not copy it mechanically.
5. Privacy Guardian checks title, body, tags, source paths, and excerpts.
6. Reviewer checks that the summary still has adequate evidence.
7. Operator activates it in shared with `--allow-shared` after the disclosure review.
8. Generate a destination packet and inspect exactly what it includes.

Example: business wants software to build a content tracker. Share the approved functional requirements and non-sensitive workflow, not full client correspondence or personal notes about the client.

The shared copy is independent. If the original changes, update or retire the shared version deliberately. There is no automatic synchronization between records.
