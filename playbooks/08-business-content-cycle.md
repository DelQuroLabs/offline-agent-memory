# Business: from objective to content and learning

Strategist states the audience, goal, and testable assumption. Content Studio drafts from an approved brief and source pack. Operations Designer defines review and publication handoffs. Analyst checks available results using explicit metric definitions. Reviewer checks factual claims and alignment with the brief.

Deliverables: a content draft, claims checklist, publication-ready review notes, and a measurement plan. The workflow does not publish, email, buy ads, or access external accounts.

Memory candidates: approved brand conventions, validated audience findings, metric definitions, and lessons tied to actual results. Draft slogans and untested performance claims remain drafts or project artifacts.
